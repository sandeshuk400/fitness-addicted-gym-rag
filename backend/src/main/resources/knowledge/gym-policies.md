# Fitness Addicted Gym policies

## Opening hours
The gym is open every day from 5:00 am to 11:00 pm.

## Joining fee
A one-time joining fee of INR 500 is charged with a member's first membership purchase. It is shown as its own payment with its own receipt. The joining fee is non-refundable. It is not charged again on later purchases.

## Refunds
A membership payment can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. After 7 days a refund is no longer possible. Refunds are handled by the gym team at the front desk.

## Freezing a membership
An active membership can be frozen for up to 30 days in total. While frozen, the membership does not count down, and the end date moves later by the number of frozen days. A queued membership that starts after it moves later too. A frozen membership can be unfrozen early.

## Renewing and buying a second plan
If you buy another plan while your membership is still active, the new plan is queued and starts the day after the current one ends. Members get reminders 7, 3 and 1 days before a membership expires.

## Payments
Memberships are paid at the front desk, and every payment gets a receipt number.

## Fingerprint door access
You can open the gym door with your fingerprint once the front desk has enrolled you. Fingerprint access is optional and you must agree to it in your profile. Your fingerprint is stored on the door device only, not in the gym's database. You can leave and come back in on the same day, and your visit is counted once per day. To leave, press the exit button. Fingerprint access only works while you have an active membership.

## Health information
Health notes in your profile are private. They are visible only to you and to gym administrators, and they are never sent to the AI assistant.
